How we keep it good

  • The host decides. Nothing runs on an event unless the host adds it.
  • Weddings and private events are ad-free unless the couple asks otherwise.
  • Nothing ever appears on a guest's camera screen while they're shooting.
  • Every placement is labeled as sponsored. No tracking pixels on film pages.
  • Contributors can share in revenue a film earns, split by their screen time.
