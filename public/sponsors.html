How VUEMesh will pay for itself

Personal shows stay free. Paid plans for organizations (permanent rooms, their own branding, longer storage, priority help) and partnerships like these keep it running and keep it independent. If that's a business you'd like to be part of, say hello.

When publishing and the public map of events open, there'll be room for a few more places a sponsor can appear: a "Presented by" card under published films, a quiet line on the beacon guests scan to join, and featured events on the map. They aren't for sale yet, because they aren't built yet.

How we keep it good

  • The host decides. Nothing runs on a show unless the host adds it.
  • Weddings and private events are ad-free unless the hosts ask otherwise.
  • Nothing ever appears on a camera's screen while it's shooting.
  • Every placement is labeled as sponsored. No tracking pixels.
  • Reviews of gear are honest, partner or not.
