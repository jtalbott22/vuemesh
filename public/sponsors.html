VUEMesh

Let's make something together.

VUEMesh is a production studio in the browser: USB cameras and mics, any screen, your clips and the phones in the room, switched live, recorded and streamed. It's built by one person who has run a lot of live events, and built it for them. If you're making something it could plug into, have gear it should work with, or want your shows seen, hit me up.

  • Cameras, screens, sound and clips in one tab
  • Nothing to install
  • Video stays on the local network
  • Free and open source (MIT)

Content

Host your shows on VUEMesh.

Real today
Publishing is built and switched off for now: a finished film goes up with its credits, and its watch page checks every contributor's signature in the browser, so anyone can see who shot what. The site runs on Amazon's content delivery network.
Let's
If you'd like your films or shows hosted and played here, we can switch it on for you and partner on the delivery: you cover what it costs to host and serve your content, and it gets a home with verified credits.

How we keep it good

  • The host decides. Nothing runs on a show unless the host adds it.
  • Weddings and private events are ad-free unless the hosts ask otherwise.
  • Nothing ever appears on a camera's screen while it's shooting.
  • Every sponsored spot is labeled as sponsored. No tracking pixels.
  • Reviews of gear are honest, partner or not.
