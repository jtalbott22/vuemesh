          OutputFrame rate, size and shape of the program

          About twice the bitrate for Record program (12 Mbit/s at 720p, 20 at 1080p) and this computer's own recordings (16, a screen 8). The camera on air may send up to 15 Mbit/s on the local network, not 8. Bigger files, and more for this computer and the Wi-Fi to carry.

          Every shot is shaped to one output, so landscape and portrait phones never mix. Fill crops to fit; whole shot adds bars.
