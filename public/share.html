Share this computer’s screen

Into the studio, as a source the director can cut to: slides, a demo, a video.

  1. Enter the code from the studio’s screen, and name this computer.
  2. Pick what you’re showing, so it stays sharp or smooth.
  3. Choose a screen, window or tab in the browser’s picker. Only that is shared.

This browser can’t share its screen (phones and tablets mostly can’t). Open this page on a computer, in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
