Not a video upload service.
A production system.

Nothing you shoot is uploaded to be processed somewhere else. VUEMesh coordinates your cameras, microphones and controls in the browser; the cloud only introduces your devices to each other, and the pictures go straight between them on your own network.

  • Browser-native No app to install. A compatible browser is the production client.
  • Distributed by design Sources communicate with the production instead of requiring every video stream to become a cloud-processing job.

Use what's already in the drawer.

      Where everything goes

      • Pictures and sound go device to device, over your own Wi‑Fi or network. They're never uploaded, and there's no server in the middle to pass them through.
      • The cloud only makes introductions: the event's code and name, who's in it, and the handshake that lets your devices find each other. Turn on Local network only in the studio and that's all that ever leaves the room.
      • Recordings stay yours. Each camera records itself in full quality and hands the file straight to your studio computer, where it stays in the browser until you export or delete it. The camera keeps its own copy.
      • Going live is your call. The show goes to YouTube or wherever you choose straight from your studio computer, and only while you're streaming.

        Nothing to install.

        A browser, cameras, microphones and a network. That's the point. VUEMesh turns the devices you already have into a coordinated production system — without asking anyone to install an app.

        • A network that connects the sources The production sources need to communicate reliably. A solid local network is more important than a huge internet connection. Venue Wi-Fi can work. A network you control is the safest bet for production.
        • A browser to run the studio Open the studio in a browser and it becomes the control room: camera views, sound, program output, director controls and recording. Chrome or Edge on a suitable computer is the simplest way to run the studio.
        • Then add your sources USB webcams and audio interfaces plug straight into the studio. Any screen shares in, from this computer or another one. Your clips are a click away, and phones and tablets join with one scan. Start with one webcam. Add angles when the show needs them.

        VUEMesh uses the cloud to coordinate sessions and connect participants. The production is designed to happen between the browsers and the host rather than requiring every video stream to pass through a central video server.

        Then make it a real control room.

        One computer can run the show. Add screens, cameras, microphones, clips or a public display and the same browser-native system scales into a full production environment.

        • Direct from your pocket Pair a phone as a remote and walk the room: cut cameras, call a break, fade to black, start the recording, all while you keep an eye on the real thing. The studio runs the show. You don't have to stand next to it.
        • Every screen in the room is a monitor Open /tv on a smart TV, phone, tablet, a spare laptop or a projector, and it shows the live program straight from the studio, browses the night's recordings, or becomes the QR Beacon guests scan. Built for the big screen, and coming to more of them.
        • More screens, more control Put the program on one display, the QR Beacon on another, and keep camera control, sound and monitoring in their own windows. The browser becomes the control room. One screen is enough to start. More screens turn the same system into a larger production desk.
        • Clips, ads and packages Drop in an intro, b-roll, a sponsor's ad or a film you made beforehand. Each waits in Sources, ready, and goes on air with a click: once and back to the camera, or looped until you cut away. Aired clips land in the edit, right where they played.
        • Breaks your way Six ready-made slates, from color bars to a station clock, with your own message and tone. Rotate your sponsors' stills and videos through every break, or air your own clip instead. If the camera on air drops, the break goes up by itself.
        • Go live anywhere Send the finished program to YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, Kick, Vimeo, LinkedIn, X, or another service that accepts RTMP or WHIP. The studio guides you through the connection. Then it's paste your stream key, press Go live.

        Put it on a TV. Bring in a screen.

        Two pages, nothing to install. /tv turns any screen into a monitor, with or without a studio. /share sends a computer's screen into a show.

        • How to use the TV page On a smart TV, projector, tablet or spare laptop, open /tv in its browser.
          1. Show a studio's program.Type the event code (it's on the studio and on every QR Beacon), name the screen, and Connect. The live program plays straight from the studio.
          2. Or skip the studio.The TV shows its own code and QR. Phones scan it; computers open /share and type the code. Up to nine show side by side, one full screen at a tap. Nothing is recorded.
          3. Switch what it shows.Once connected, choose Program, Browse the night's recordings, QR Beacon for guests to scan, or PIP for the program in a corner.
          Open the TV page
        • How to use the Share page On the presenter's computer, open /share in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Phones and tablets mostly can't share a screen.
          1. Enter the code.The studio's event code, or a TV's own code to show the screen on that TV.
          2. Name it and share.Slides and text are sent sharp, every word readable; when a video plays, it turns smooth, 30 frames a second, by itself.
          3. Share a screen.Pick a screen, window or tab, with its sound. Keep the tab open behind your slides; the tally tells you when you're next or on air.

        The films up top were made on one gaming PC.

        Nobody filmed that wedding. First Dance, Live Tonight and Game Night are AI-made: 28 shots, written as prompts, drawn and brought to life by open models running at home, then cut, scored and titled by a script. The recipe is open, so you can make your own.

        3. Pick the bestA review page: click the take you like.You
        4. Bring it to lifeEach picked still becomes a moving shot.LTX image-to-video
        5. Score itMusic written to the exact length of the cut.ACE-Step
        6. Cut itTrims, dissolves, titles, a grade and the music.ffmpeg

        What it runs on

        An NVIDIA gaming PC, with ComfyUI in Docker drawing the pictures, motion and music, and a Jupyter notebook driving it all, one cell at a time. No cloud, no subscription, no upload: the same idea as VUEMesh, pointed at making films.
