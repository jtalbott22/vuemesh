# Code of Conduct

VUEMesh is made by people from different crafts: video, sound, theatre, music, teaching, software.
We want everyone who joins in to feel welcome, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project, to code, or to live
  production.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you conclude.
- Critique work, not people. Say what would make it better.
- Credit others' ideas and work.
- Respect people's privacy, in the project and in what you build with it. Never share someone's
  footage, logs or personal details without their permission.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or abilities.
- Sexual language or imagery aimed at people, or unwelcome attention.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to do these things.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer privately at info@vuemesh.com. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers
may remove comments, code or issues, and may block people from the project, to keep it a good place
to work.

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.
